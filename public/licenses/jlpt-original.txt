KanaLearn — Original reading exercises
Revision: 2026-10-28.original-reading.en.v2

The five Japanese reading passages, their questions, English translations and
explanations in content/jlpt/readings.json are original AI-assisted contributions.
They remain unreviewed by a human Japanese-language reviewer. Automated checks
verify structure and exact evidence references, not naturalness or exam difficulty.

The former Indonesian vocabulary/grammar pilot has been replaced by the attributed
OpenJLPT corpus. Historical user session snapshots are retained unchanged.
This MIT notice applies only to the original reading exercises. Imported OpenJLPT
vocabulary/grammar and derived drills retain CC BY-SA 4.0 and upstream notices.
No audio is included. These are unofficial learning exercises, not released exams.
